# Kinetic caption system

## Zones

| Zone | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `bottom` | Conversational phrases over lower chest or beneath a PiP |
| `top` | Large hook/action text in genuine upper negative space |
| `mid` | Short bridge phrases when product UI leaves a clear center lane |
| `depth` | High-value phrase behind an alpha-matted presenter |

Do not put upper captions over titles, UI cards, phone previews, or platform search chrome. Revert to bottom when no safe negative space exists.

## Semantic treatment

| Meaning | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Hook, payoff, strong action | Impact |
| Process or two-level phrase | Stacked |
| Number, imperative, question | Marker |
| Connective explanation | Minimal |
| Product or technical noun | Standard with restrained accent |

## Timing

- 1–5 words per phrase;
- 0.5–2.2 seconds per group;
- word activation at transcript timestamps;
- 0.12–0.25 second entrance;
- 0.08–0.15 second exit;
- hard `visibility:hidden` or opacity kill at group end.

## Motion

Use scale/color activation for ordinary words and a stronger overshoot for the semantic emphasis word. Keep motion deterministic and seek-safe. Do not hash words into arbitrary colors, rotations, or zones.

## Depth hierarchy

Depth text should be 80–130 px in a 1080×1920 frame, usually transparent/no card, with enough contrast against the plate. Place it so the presenter hides 10–45% of the phrase, not the entire key word.

## Platform safety

Keep essential shared Reels/TikTok copy below the top UI exclusion area and above bottom controls. A roaming caption may enter decorative areas only when its content is duplicated safely elsewhere or is nonessential.
